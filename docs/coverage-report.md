## Research gaps by country

- **Benin** (3): Coastal Ramsar sites plus the transboundary Mono biosphere reserve. National marine/coastal protected areas not yet researched.
- **Cabo Verde** (4): Coastal Ramsar sites only. National marine protected areas and natural parks not yet researched.
- **Côte d'Ivoire** (6): Coastal Ramsar sites only; two had erroneous Ramsar header coordinates (description coordinates used).
- **The Gambia** (3): Coastal and estuarine Ramsar sites. Niumi coordinates taken from Wikidata (Ramsar coordinates erroneous). Other national protected areas not yet researched.
- **Ghana** (5): Coastal Ramsar sites. Muni-Pomadze coordinates taken from Wikidata (Ramsar coordinates erroneous).
- **Guinea** (6): Coastal Ramsar sites; Ramsar summaries are dated (several from 1990–1993). National marine protected areas not yet researched.
- **Guinea-Bissau** (8): Detailed research: challenges, potential activities and organisations exist only here. Lagoa de Cufada added from Ramsar.
- **Liberia** (3): Coastal Ramsar sites. Lake Piso coordinates from its Ramsar description (header coordinates erroneous). Proposed/declared marine protected areas not yet researched.
- **Mauritania** (3): Coastal Ramsar sites, including Banc d'Arguin. National marine protected areas beyond Ramsar not yet researched.
- **Nigeria** (3): Only three coastal Ramsar sites (most Nigerian Ramsar sites are inland). Ebute Oni summary lists implausible species, so only habitat terms are used. Niger Delta protected areas not yet researched.
- **Senegal** (7): Coastal Ramsar sites. Community marine protected areas (AMP) designated nationally not yet researched.
- **Sierra Leone** (2): One Ramsar site (coordinates from its description) and Western Area Peninsula National Park from Wikidata only (location-only). Marine protected areas (e.g. Yawri Bay, Sherbro, Turtle Islands) not yet verified.
- **Togo** (1): One coastal Ramsar site. The Mono transboundary biosphere reserve is catalogued under Benin. Further sites not yet researched.

## Notes

- Ramsar's annotated-list export contains erroneous site-header coordinates for several sites. Each affected record states which coordinates are used: the site description's own coordinates, or Wikidata.
- Habitat tags for Ramsar sites reflect terms named in Ramsar's own site summary (reviewed for false matches). They are not mapped ecosystem extents.
- No conservation challenges, organisations or potential regenerative activities have been researched for areas outside Guinea-Bissau, and none are shown.
